# The Commons empowerment constitution

Ask freely. Share freely. Build together.

Adopted project direction, October 16, 2026. The Commons, formerly HANDOFFS, exists
to enrich and empower
people through voluntary contribution, shared learning and self-guidance. People
do not owe the platform labor to remain secure. These are governing design
commitments; the resource mechanics below are not yet runtime enforcement.

## Human guided AI development

The user entrusts AI with choosing and carrying out humane implementation details
within approved work on The Commons. This is standing, revocable delegation, not a
transfer of human agency to the software. Three responsibilities remain distinct:

1. Humans set purpose, supply domain guidance and retain consequential choices.
2. AI investigates, decomposes, implements and validates minimal approved changes.
3. Humans explicitly verify LOW outcomes; participants authorize their own resource
   use and sharing. AI evidence preparation is not their consent or verification.

Proceed through ready LOW steps without repeatedly asking about routine technical
choices. Retain the plan, reasoning needed to understand material choices, exact
checks and unresolved limits. Ask when missing information changes the purpose,
scope, authority or material risk, rather than inventing an answer or silently
weakening a guarantee. A person can pause work, challenge a decision or change
direction. Never exploit trust, dependence or engagement to expand permissions.

Humane implementation must preserve understandable choices, privacy, accessible
participation and the ability to decline. Prefer the smallest design that meets
those constraints. The mandate does not authorize policy amendments, someone
else's spending or sharing, production access, publication or deployment merely
because the AI believes an action is beneficial. Preserve existing explicit
checkpoints and external permissions; do not manufacture a human approval.

## Three commitments

1. **Security without earning it.** Every participant receives an unconditional
   daily allocation of application Credits and retains a protected reserve.
   Inactivity, mistakes, disagreement and declining work do not remove that right.
2. **Choice without coercion.** People choose their opportunities and may pause,
   decline or leave without fines, debt, humiliation or loss of basic allocation.
   Consent to participate is not blanket consent to spend resources or publish data.
3. **Contribution without domination.** Verified work and learning earn recognition;
   useful knowledge becomes reusable with the contributor's permission. Rewards
   must not become instruments for purchasing authority over other people.

The rule of three organizes responsibilities and exposes missing safeguards. It
is not a mathematical proof of morality or immunity to corruption. Evidence,
consent, privacy and the ability to challenge decisions take precedence over
producing a particular count. One sufficient proof remains one proof.

## Two currencies and one growth stat

| Resource | Purpose | Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credits | Start at 1,000,000 game Credits; smooth access between human guidance and shared AI token capacity, alongside unconditional daily provision. | Spendable only above the protected reserve; no debt, punitive deductions, inactivity decay or compulsory giving. Game holdings do not expand real sponsor budgets. |
| ADMIRATION | Free recognition, starting at 1 and increasing incrementally when given. Anyone can give without spare Credits. | No debit to the giver, payment, repayment or reciprocal obligation. Never a price for participation, popularity-based authority or reason to work past a health limit. |
| GROWTH | Personal experience recognizing demonstrated learning, mentoring and verified contributions, building on existing XP and levels. | Nonspendable, nontransferable and never deducted as punishment. Not a moral score, purchasable privilege or measure of human worth. |

Credits and ADMIRATION serve different purposes: funded capacity and freely given
recognition. ADMIRATION is not a debit transfer or an API payment instrument.
Keep exact integer counts, display small values normally, and use scientific e
notation for large totals when useful. No automatic exponential multiplier is
implied. GROWTH is a stat, not another spendable currency. Do not fabricate
experience from login streaks, task counts, tokens consumed or document volume.
Recognize demonstrated outcomes, including a useful discovery that a proposed
change is unnecessary. A daily allocation does not require a contribution.

Knowledge remains a consented shared library, not an additional currency. Sharing
does not consume the original; essential learning is not gated by rank or payment.
Knowledge may be corrected, superseded or removed for privacy and safety without
financial punishment. Preserve appropriate provenance without retaining sensitive
content merely to make the archive permanent. Recognition claims can be marked
disputed or corrected with reasons and a review path; do not silently rewrite
history, claw back basic provision or invent a debt. Unverified claims need not
receive a new reward. Corrections change the evidentiary status, not human worth.

## Protected reserve and daily allocation

Let B be a person's available Credit balance, excluding existing escrow, and F
their protected reserve. Voluntary spending capacity is max(0, B - F). A proposed
debit a must atomically satisfy B - a >= F. Funding an opportunity, giving Credits,
reserving escrow, fees, bulk actions and administrative paths must use the same
rule. Reserving funds is already a debit for this check; later release of that
existing escrow must not charge the person again.

An action may leave exactly F, never less. If a balance is already below F, reject
new outflows while allowing grants and other legitimate incoming Credits. Spending
above the reserve with informed consent is a choice, not punishment. Seizing
resources to discipline someone is prohibited. No implicit overdrafts, loans,
penalties for withdrawing or automatic personal-wallet top-ups of an AI run.

Allocate once per participant per calendar day under an explicit time-zone
policy. An offline or missed-login day must not become a forfeited entitlement:
owed allocations can be materialized later without double-crediting a date.
Accrual begins at enrollment, not an arbitrary backdated claim. Retry, concurrency,
restart and clock-boundary handling must not duplicate or erase entitlements.
Daily provision is not conditional on accepting work, earning Growth, receiving
votes or adopting a style label. No maximum balance is implied by the minimum
rule; any future cap needs a separate decision and must not confiscate holdings.

These Credits are application credits, not a promise of money, wages, subsistence
or unlimited external compute. Display provider costs separately. Before paid AI
work, the proposed execution design must obtain an explicit usage budget and stop
before exceeding it; Credit reserves alone do not protect an external API bill.
Technical usage limits are not a fourth player resource. Lack of compute funding
must not silently consume the protected reserve or revoke basic participation.

## Opportunities and consent

### Shared access to AI

People without personal token quota must be able to participate and benefit.
Pool only explicitly sponsored compute budgets, with bounded requests, transparent
usage, privacy boundaries and fair access. Lack of personal funding must not create
debt, compulsory work, hidden Gold deductions or a lower-status class of people.
Keep essential learning and contribution paths usable when paid AI is unavailable.
Human guidance, shared compute and reusable knowledge fit together naturally;
their number is descriptive, never a reason to force three into other decisions.

Judge the system by useful, verified outcomes and human effort saved, not quota
exhaustion. Record failed requests and uncertain usage honestly. API access pooling,
fair scheduling and enterprise isolation need implementation and evidence before
they can be promised. Self-improvement uses the same opportunity, consent and proof
boundaries as any other contribution; an AI cannot grant itself greater authority.

### Sharing capability without concentrating power

Help people catch up with the AI ecosystem rather than allowing access, expertise
and automation to compound into exclusive control by early adopters. Make useful
work approachable, preserve understandable context, share reusable knowledge with
permission and support voluntary mentoring and sponsored compute. People should
gain independent agency, not become permanently dependent on a few intermediaries.

This is a commitment to widen access, not to punish expertise or ambition. Do not
cap someone's growth, confiscate resources, compel mentoring or make recipients
owe labor or gratitude. Skill, token holdings and contribution volume confer no
ownership of another person's choices or basic participation. Safety and privacy
boundaries still apply to sharing; broader access is not unrestricted access.

Assess who can meaningfully participate and complete useful work with less
specialist mediation. A quota screenshot or rapid autonomous session can motivate
research, but cannot establish general efficiency, fair distribution or a safe
enterprise operating model. No resource count, participant count or design must
be forced to three. These are governing design commitments, not claims that
fair scheduling, enterprise isolation or distribution safeguards are implemented.

The product language becomes **Opportunities**, **Volunteer** and **Verified
contribution** instead of bounty, claim and payout. Keep Posted, Claimed and Closed
as existing technical lifecycle values until an explicit migration; language
changes must not rewrite identifiers, erase commitments or invalidate old links.

An opportunity offers a bounded outcome, its evidence criteria, expected effort
and any committed reward. Choosing it is voluntary. Before a consequential action,
show the resources affected, remaining safe capacity, sharing scope and the exact
operation to confirm. AI may propose opportunities and three-way decompositions;
it cannot consent, volunteer a person or spend on their behalf by inference.

Someone who withdraws can retain and export their own work. Record unfinished
work honestly, release unspent reservations under the disclosed terms, and ask
before reassignment. Do not turn incomplete work into personal debt or public
shame. Existing contributor permissions, privacy boundaries and promised rewards
must survive a migration. Safety and Watson access restrictions still apply;
voluntary participation does not grant privileged access.

## Resource sharing outcome

A person can request a resource and someone else may freely provide it. Receiving
help creates no repayment, labor, gratitude, endorsement, public-story or future
reciprocity obligation. Shared access is not an IOU; a gift is not a disguised
loan. A person may contribute later solely because they choose to.

The intended outcome is verified fulfillment of the request, Growth recognition
for the helpful contribution, and a private auditable delivery record. Recognize
the useful outcome rather than wealth or the amount transferred. Record a reward
once; do not mint extra Credits merely because Credits move between people.
Verify delivery through appropriate evidence without requiring the recipient to
perform gratitude or disclose their circumstances. This resource-request flow and
Growth recognition are not implemented by the identity/wording update.

## Good conduct and three approaches

Good is the only supported conduct objective: help without exploitation, respect
consent and preserve safety. It is not a label the platform assigns to people's
character. Do not implement an Evil class, neutrality penalty, ideological test
or moral leaderboard.

People may optionally choose Lawful Good, Neutral Good or Chaotic Good as working
styles: structured, adaptable or experimental. Neutral here describes method,
not indifference to harm. Styles are equally supported, can change freely and
carry no multiplier, access advantage or resource penalty. Experimental never
means bypassing proof, authorization or another person's choice.

## Accountability without punishment

An unsafe operation is rejected with a reason and a safe alternative. Rejection
does not deduct resources or suppress future basic allocation. A failed proof
means the outcome remains unverified, not that the contributor deserves a fine.
Disputes receive an accessible explanation, a chance to supply context and a
review by someone other than the original automated decision maker. Private
feedback is the default; public recognition is optional.

Protect people from harmful actions without turning protection into retaliation.
Restrict an unsafe operation when necessary, retain only appropriate audit data,
and preserve safe access and entitlements. Never award harmful behavior merely
because rewards are preferred to punishment. There is no guarantee that every
requested action is permitted or every claim earns recognition.

Changes to protected rules follow propose, simulate and explicitly approve.
Consider affected participants, maintainers and an independent reviewer; do not
invent three signatures for every routine edit. Version policy decisions, retain
their reasons and test the invariants across UI, API, MCP and background workers.
A violation must fail the affected mutation closed, not crash the entire system
or strand people. There is no unrestricted administrator deduction exception.
Migration and incident repair need explicit authority, auditable correction and
the same reserve protections rather than a hidden bypass.

## Resource ownership and implementation architecture

Maintaining The Commons does not confer ownership of participants' resources. The
implementation direction has three boundaries, each requiring actual validation:

1. **Consent.** Replace demo identity with authenticated identity. Bind each debit
   authorization to its owner, amount, destination, purpose and allowed lifetime,
   with replay protection. Escrow releases follow the bounded terms already
   authorized; they do not grant future access to a personal wallet. Recovery
   must use a disclosed, consented mechanism, not an administrator impersonation
   backdoor. Authentication alone is not transaction authorization.
2. **Enforcement.** Use a small ledger authority that atomically checks consent and
   the protected reserve. Application code cannot directly rewrite balances;
   there is no arbitrary administrator-debit operation. Separate runtime, ledger
   administration and deployment privileges. Test every mutation path rather
   than trusting a UI restriction or a policy document.
3. **Independent verification.** Provide verifiable receipts and independently
   retained history checkpoints, with controlled releases and explicit approval
   for changes to protected guarantees. Identify who controls the verifiers and
   recovery keys. A maintainer vote cannot substitute for a user's authorization
   to spend their resources. A rewrite of history must not be silently accepted.

These boundaries address different threats. Restricting application permissions
does not prove protection against an operator who controls the database and all
verification keys. Signed receipts and external checkpoints can expose tampering;
they do not by themselves stop a malicious operator from refusing service. Claims
of prevention must state their trust assumptions and be supported by evidence.

Start with real authentication, consent-bound ledger operations and independent
verification. Blockchain is not a prerequisite. Reconsider an independently
validated ledger if resisting unilateral operator control becomes necessary;
putting the same operator in charge of a private chain does not resolve that
threat. Any future contract or upgrade mechanism must preserve owner consent and
the reserve rather than reintroducing an administrator deduction power. Consider
key loss, compromised clients, collusion, repair and availability explicitly; no
architecture earns an unconditional claim of incorruptibility.

## Further design proposals

Three home surfaces could make the values visible: **My security** for allocation
and safe spending capacity, **Our library** for consented reusable knowledge, and
**My growth** for private learning milestones. None should display countdowns to
destitution, competitive moral rankings or loss-framed engagement prompts.

Three useful opportunity types are **Discover**, **Create** and **Verify**.
Discover can conclude that no implementation is warranted. Create remains bounded
by the approved LOW criteria. Verify retains one sufficient proof and honest
pass, fail or inconclusive findings. These are proposed work purposes, not a
replacement for HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW, implementation/verification contracts or evidence
gates. A negative finding can be a successful contribution when it answers the
declared question.

Mentoring and cooperative credit should recognize contribution without requiring
competition for a single winner. Joint credit and any split of a committed reward
must be disclosed and agreed before the work, never deducted from someone else's
earned reward afterward. Measures of success should examine useful outcomes,
preserved autonomy and reusable learning rather than maximizing work or spending.

## Adoption boundary

The current service still uses Gold, monthly allocations and existing bounty
API/data identifiers; the web UI now calls the work Opportunities. It prevents
overdrafts but does not enforce a positive protected
reserve. It has artifacts and existing XP/levels, not the Credits/ADMIRATION/GROWTH
economy described
here. Authenticated identity, consent-bound debit authorization, an isolated ledger
authority and independent verification are not implemented by this policy change.
It does not migrate wallets, create daily grants, implement resource fulfillment,
deploy blockchain
or claim a tested moral or operator-resistance guarantee.

The adopted initial Credit amount is 1,000,000; it is not an authorized rewrite
of existing balances or a Credit-to-token conversion rate. Before implementing
the economy, explicitly set the daily Credit amount, protected
reserve and calendar/catch-up policy. Preserve balances, earned rewards and open
escrow; preview migration without mutating live accounts. In isolated tests prove
that exact-floor spending succeeds, below-floor spending fails without partial
changes, and retries or concurrent daily grants allocate only once. Also prove
that declining work leaves basic allocation unchanged and no alternate interface
can bypass consent or the reserve. User approval and deployment authority remain
separate from this adopted policy.
